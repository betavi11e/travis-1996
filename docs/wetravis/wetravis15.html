When Travis returned George put her under strict curfew, which involved Mary Frances escorting her to and from school...


...and George bolting her bedroom window from the outside.


But during a lunch break Travis managed to call Candy from a pay phone, and they became friends, talking every day.

One lunch hour Candy picked Travis up in a Ding-a-Ling cab that then took them both to West Egg station.

They walked arm-in-arm to the Manhattan side of the platform, bound for Times Square.
