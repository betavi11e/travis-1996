When they returned to their more temperate island, George and Mary Frances put Travis into the concerned hands of Judith Horowitz, a teen guidance counselor at West Egg Junior High.


However Travis remained resistant to Ms. Horowitz's administrations...


... at one stage even leaping off her chair to throw a copy of "Catcher In The Rye" at the unfortunate woman.


For in fact, Travis was receiving counsel elsewhere, in the form of her mother's brother, Bruno Anthony.
