It only took Travis three days in San Francisco to organize his fake I.D.


Some Chinese guy not only gave him a passport, drivers license and social security number, but a military record as well (Marines, honorable discharge).


Travis thought he remembered enough grunt talk from Saigon to pull it off.

He hot-wired an old Studebaker and set off for Iris' New York.

He picked up hitchers for company, but in case of trouble, he kept a .44 taped under the dash.
