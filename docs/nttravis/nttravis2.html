Travis was taken home to South Alligator, where George supervised a uranium mine.

A sorry collection of corrugated iron five hundred miles from Darwin, and perhaps another thousand from the twentieth century.

George and Mary Frances had already sired a son, Harry, eighteen months earlier. But after Travis, Mary Frances had a series of miscarriages.

Later she would blame high levels of radiation for her malfunctioning womb, but the immediate fallout was a six year gap between Travis and his sister Sally...

...and a mother who spent most of her waking hours horizontal.
