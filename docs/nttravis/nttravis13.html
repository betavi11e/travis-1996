Back in Bangkok Travis took up with an older woman, an ex-New Yorker named Iris who tended bar at one of the clubs on Patpong Road.

She would talk endlessly to Travis about the beauty of the neon-lit Times Square...


...colored steam vaporising through manhole covers and more shows than you could go to in a lifetime.

People who met Travis in those years took him for an American. He may well have adopted this persona as a way of throwing his parents off the scent...

...if indeed they were still sniffing him out.
